<u>Morning</u>
- Whenever you wake up (~7-9am), take him on a little walk around the backyard. He'll need to poop.
- His breakfast is **half a cup** (the cup that's already in the dog food bin) of dog food and a couple spoonfuls of the canned dog food.
- Take Yommi and his full food bowl back to his spot in the home office.
- Leash him in the home office (outside the crate) for his breakfast.

<u>Night</u>
- At <u>5pm</u>, it's his dinner. His dinner is the same as his breakfast – half a cup of dog food and a couple spoonfuls of the canned dog food.
- After eating his dinner, let him play outside (no leash). He'll be done playing outside when he barks at the backyard door for you to come get him.
- Leash him. He will automatically want to go to his spot in the living room. Leash him to the red leash connected to the glass table. Give him a jerky treat after leashing him.
- He will want little snacks throughout his stay in the living room.
- At <u>9pm</u>, that's time for him to pee. Prepare a jerky treat and a little bit of the dental green stick (1/3 or 1/4 of the stick). Leash him and walk him to the backyard door. He'll want to go through the kitchen because he likes to scoop up little scraps on the ground.
- You just walk with him in the backyard until he pees, then you can head back inside.
- Grab the prepared treats and walk with him to his crate in the home office.
- Before putting him in the crate, make sure his blanket is all nice, making sure it covers the floor of the crate. Make sure his water bowl in the crate is full.
- Now, you can put him in the crate! Give him the biscuit treat first, then the dental treat. 
- If his food bowl is still in the home office, you can put it back on the metal gray stand near the backyard door so it's ready to be filled for the next day.
