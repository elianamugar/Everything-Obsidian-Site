# Notes
- Old French *estiquette* – to attach or stick
- "Whether it be in relation to speech, timeliness, body language or dining, politeness is key."
- Courteousness at all times
- Reservedness – overfamiliarity of personal space/behavior is a big no-no
	- Asking personal questions is frowned upon
- importance on punctuality
	- rude to arrive late
- Dinner guests bring gift for the host
	- Good table manners essential
		- wait until everyone at the table has been served/host starts eating/indicates that you should do so
- Social classes
	- rules of etiquette are usually unwritten and passed down from generation to generation
	- etiquette was often used as a social weapon in the interests of social advancement or exclusion
- evolution of etiquette
	- corporate etiquette - shift in focus from social/household setting to an emphasis on business etiquette and protocol
