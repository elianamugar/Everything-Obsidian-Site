#### 10.0 Positivism, Critical Theory, and Postmodern Understandings
- critiques of [[Positivism]] IR theorizing by [[critical theory]] and [[postmodernism]]/[[post-structuralism]]
	- the critique relies on interpretive understanding
	- [[critical theory]] –> [[post-positivism]]
	- [[postmodernism]] –> [[post-structuralism]]
	- their critiques underscore the subjectivity of human inquiry and reject notions of pure objectivity/value-free science associated w/[[Positivism]]
		- [[critical theory]], [[constructivism]], [[feminism]] have not completely strayed away from science
			- use of empirical evidence in case studies
- science is open to scholarship that incorporates interpretive understandings along with the canons of logic and evidence that are central to [[Positivism]]
	- thin line between [[critical theory]] and [[postmodernism]]
		- linguistic analysis in [[postmodernism]]
			- has clear place in [[critical theory]] that scrutinizes "cover stories" and unpacks/deconstructs the language used and roles played by those in positions of power
- observations in IR and other social/natural sciences are heavily influenced by interpretive understandings of the concepts employed
	- many critiques/interpretive understandings are informed by [[Phenomenology]]
		- a subjective/interpretive understanding in human consciousness of what we observe/think we see in the world around us
