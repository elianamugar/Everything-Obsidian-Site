#### 10.6.0 Queer Theory – Thinking Critically about IR
- less impact on IR, new field
	- [[feminism]] ([[Cynthia Weber]]) more open than others to discuss sexuality
- LGBTQ+ long history in humankind across culture
	- these "labels" are social constructions, found in present-day understandings of fluidity of sexuality and gender identities
- queer studies – human security focus on discrimination, marginalization, violence toward queer individuals and communities globally
	- worse in some regions/countries than others
- intersection of cultural biases and religious teachings against LGBTQ constitute a toxic environment dangerous to queer persons
	- human rights, human security, global challenge
- queer theory
	- goes beyond human rights, human security, global challenge issues
		- probes how sexual orientation and gender identity relate to understandings in IR
	- subset of [[critical theory]]
		- [[Foucault]]
			- [[History of Sexuality by Michel Foucault]]
	- challenges binaries
		- war/peace, powerful/weak, just/unjust, developed/undeveloped, democratic/authoritarian
		- IR more complex than being reduced to either/or binaries
- [[sovereignty]]
	- sexualized as a form of male dominance
	- masculinist orientation
		- Melanie [[Richter-Montpetit]] notes [[Richard Ashley]]'s observation that identifies *statecraft* under the claim of sovereignty as a form of *mancraft*
			- [[Peace of Westphalia]] system of sovereign states is really one of sovereign man
			- the emerging “queer turn” in IR extends and reworks critical IR epistemologies, ontologies and methodologies. Engaging with queer scholarship will further expand and refine the notion of the political in IR, and help produce more complex and robust understandings of the operations of contemporary formations of international power, including beyond unhelpful binaries of power and resistance. #quote 
		- [[Richter-Montpetit]] notes how [[Cynthia Weber]] goes beyond [[Foucault]]
			- "Weber explores how Man is fundamentally constructed in relationship to sexuality... and how 'sexualized sovereign man' then intersects with race and gives rise to particular racialized figures ('the Al Qaeda terrorist')." #quote 
- the direction of queer theory
	- unclear where it'll take us in relation to IR
	- how much does sexuality matter as an explanatory variable?
	- will queer theory take us past where feminist work on masculinist orientations has brought us?
