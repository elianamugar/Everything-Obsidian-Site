#### 2.0 The Ancients – Greek, Chinese, and Indian Thought
- Roots of realism in ancient Greek and Roman writers
- Plato and Aristotle focused mostly on domestic policies
	- identified norms of right conduct by leaders and other citizens within a republic, polis, or city-state – the rule of law an ideal in itself
- **Plato:** sought a just society in an ideal republic
- **Machiavelli:** saw the security of city-state/republic dependent on good laws (political) and good arms (military), applied domestically and internationally
- Roots of liberal thought also present in Greek writers, but application was contained within the bounds of a city-state
- late-Greek and Roman Stoic writers developed notions of a community of humans that transcended the boundaries of individual units
	- provides conceptual basis for applying principles within a city-state/republic to larger world
- Roots of Marxism and neo-Marxism (re: understandings of social classes within society) have ancient roots
	- Plato and Aristotle on social harmony to avoid class conflict, a labor theory of value, and a defense of slavery
		- Marx referred to slavery as an ancient mode of production
	- Marx applied this to capitalism, seeing materially based classes as transcending state boundaries, not being confined to them
- Economic structuralism –> Marx class focus –> Plato's *Republic* –> Aristotle's defense of slavery as an economic necessity for the rulers and citizens
