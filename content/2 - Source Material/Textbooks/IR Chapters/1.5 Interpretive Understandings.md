#### 1.5.0 Interpretive Understandings
- **[[Max Weber]]:** "all knowledge of cultural reality is always knowledge from particular points of view"
	- each individual's work will be influenced by a particular doctrine, image of the world, ideology, paradigm, theory, understanding, or perspective
	- identify how this subjective, human dimension affects scholarship
	- **Verstehen:** interpretive understanding
- **Constructivists** see states and nonstate actors not as mere products of world politics, the int'l system, or an int'l/world society, but rather as actually playing a decisive role in shaping it ([[constructivism]])
- **[[Phenomenology]]:** the phenomena we experience and the subjectivity that defines the essence of human beings
- **Postmodernists** do not focus on some "objective" reality to be discovered, but rather explore the ways human beings "in here" both construct or give meanings to objects, actions, or behaviors and employ narratives or stories that convey these meanings in what is essentially a subjective approach to understanding ([[postmodernism]])
- **Feminists** highlight the dominance/exclusivity of masculinist understandings of the world around us that profoundly influence much theoretical work in the IR field ([[feminism]])
- **Green Theory:** strands of thought drawn from constructivism, critical theory, and feminism
- **Normative Theory:** prescriptive, ideal standards for how systems, behaviors, or institutions _should_ operate, focusing on "ought" rather than "is"
