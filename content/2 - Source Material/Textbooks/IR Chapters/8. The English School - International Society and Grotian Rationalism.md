#### 8.0 The English School - International Society and Grotian Rationalism
- [[The English School]]'s primary research task: trace the history and development of int'l society and uncover its nature and functioning
	- blend of 
		- [[Realism]] understandings of power and [[balance of power]]
		- [[Liberalism]] perspective on ways int'l law, rules, norms, institutions operate internationally
	- middle path (*via media*) between two traditions
- influenced by [[Machiavelli]], [[Hobbes]], [[Grotius]], [[Kant]]
- methodology
	- emphasizes historical-sociological approach
	- interpretive understandings that reflect deep skepticism about scientific and causal approach to [[IR]] theorizing
- 1950s: formal origins of [[The English School]]
	- [[post-Cold War]]
- [[The English School]]'s nuanced conceptual eclecticism well-suited to an era of change and [[Globalization]]
	- an "underutilized research resource. The time is ripe to develop and apply its historicism, constructivist, and methodologically pluralist approach to [[IR]]." #quote
