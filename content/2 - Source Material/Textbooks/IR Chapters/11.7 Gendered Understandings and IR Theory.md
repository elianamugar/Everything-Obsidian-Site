#### 11.7.0 Gendered Understandings and IR Theory
- gender as an interpretive lens
	- more aware of inequality and patterns of dominance
	- offers counterweight to masculinist understandings that are more prevalent in IR theories emphasizing power, [[balance of power]], and [[rationalism]] in conduct of state and non-state institutions (and their agents)
- principal focus: interpretive understandings associated with gender
	- critical of theoretical work in IR that masks the masculine or overlooks the feminine
- liberal feminists comfortable in [[Positivism]]
	- introducing gender as an important albeit frequently overlooked/neglected explanatory factor in IR/other social-scientific work
