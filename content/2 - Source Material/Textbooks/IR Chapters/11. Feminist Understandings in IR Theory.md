#### 11.0 Feminist Understandings in IR Theory
- interpretive understanding includes [[Liberalism]], [[radicalism]], [[postmodernism]]
- methodologies vary
- argument: IR discipline falls into the trap of believing that the masculine experience is the human experience
	- [[normative]] commitment to enhancing prospects of peace and reducing violence/conflict
		- violence and conflict often suffered by women
