#### 12.4.0 Green Theory and Their **Critics**
- like [[feminism]], critics are hard to find and most people are silent/ignore green theory
	- who would wish to make the case that concerns over the environment and future of the planet are overblown?
- inference of a critique of green theory
	- concerns over the climate and environment are recognized
	- to address these challenges, even if existential, will be achieved through the existing state system
		- including states *and* int'l orgs to which they belong
		- dealing with the environment as a collective action problem also involves NGOs and role of scientific [[epistemic communities]]
- desperate need for global shift in consciousness to ecocentric green perspective due to dangers of global environment is a hard sell to those focused on short-term economic and other gains, here and now
	- incremental approach
		- states remain preeminent actors
			- will continue to address collective action challenges through prism of calculations and self-interest
				- might take catastrophic climate event to change calculations
- many green theorists and environmentalists think the planet may be beyond recovery
