#### 10.9.0 Summation
- Positivism has occupied the mainstream of IR theory
	- modernist science and the scientific method
		- combines logic of rational theorizing and empirical testing
- recently, [[Max Weber]]'s concept of *[[Verstehen]]* and [[Phenomenology]] is at the root of interpretive understandings in critical theory and postmodern thought
	- focus on human consciousness as essential to our coming to know the world of which we're a part of
- if the central question of [[Epistemology]] is how we know what we think we know
	- critical theorists and postmodernists
		- sets aside many abstract [[universalism]] claims of logical positivists
		- focuses on human perception and understandings that give diverse meanings to concepts and theories we formulate and behaviors we observe
		- some extremists reject the scientific/modernist project and the truth claims associated with it
		- others eek to temper blanket claims of objectivity with interpretive understanding
			- subjectivity and intersubjectivity that necessarily are a part of what humans observe, think, do
