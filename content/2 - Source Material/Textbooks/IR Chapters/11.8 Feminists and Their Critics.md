#### 11.8.2 Research Program and Cumulative Knowledge
- Robert O. [[Keohane]]
	- sympathetic observer of feminist literature
		- calls for dialogue across paradigms
	- aspects of feminist understanding could fit in [[neoliberal institutionalism]]
- missing from feminist research program
	- producing cumulative knowledge about IR
- [[Tickner]]'s "You Just Don't Understand: Troubled Engagements between Feminist and Ir Theorists"
	- IR-trained feminists frequently encounter awkward silences when presenting academic papers at conferences
	- difficulty in cross-cultural conversations is due to very different realities, epistemologies, and research interests of feminists and mainstream IR scholars
		- these differences are gendered, making communication more difficult
- conventional IR scholars misunderstand feminist meaning of gender that emphasizes the socially constructed nature of gender and the fact that it embodies relationships of power inequality
- feminists and non-feminists ontologically see different realities when looking at int'l politics
	- feminists look at unequal social-power relations across levels of analysis
	- [[Realism]] looks at rational, unified states as the most important players
- epistemological divide for feminists questioning positivist approach to knowledge
	- committed to epistemological [[pluralism]]
		- many feminists are still skeptical of methodologies that claim fats are neutral
			- prefer more historically based, interpretive understandings that raise question of extent to which gender roles and patriarchy are variable across time and space
