# Thoughts
- This feels like Owen's short film - Safe cracking. Also feels like *Close*. So, you know what, since this came in 2022, it's like a mixture of Godland (2022), especially with the 4:3 aspect ratio, and Close (2022). How funny is that?
- Oh, to live in the Italian alps with THOSE FITS!
- Beautifully shot. The entire things feels like a North Face ad.
- Pietro doesn't want Bruno to go to school with him. Why does Pietro want to keep Bruno in the alps? He doesn't want to share Bruno with the world? Their sacred secret bond up in the alps.
- Enjoyable if you're looking for "living in the Italian alps" and "backpacking in the Himalayas" simulator.
- Love this shot: ![[Eight Mountains 1.png]]
- This film is primarily about Bruno and Pietro's friendship from their childhood to adulthood and how they grow as individuals with an underlying theme of fatherhood.
- At the end of the film, Bruno disappears. I actually think this is the perfect ending for his character – he was trapped in his life, with no way out, yet the only escape is... to disappear (whether to nature or his own hand).
