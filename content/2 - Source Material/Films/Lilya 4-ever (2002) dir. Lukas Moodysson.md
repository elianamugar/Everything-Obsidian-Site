# Thoughts
- Talk about an explosive opening! I just took a sip of my Dr. Pepper and I almost choked on it in the best way possible!
- The main actor kinda looks like Emma D'Arcy and Carey Mulligan.
- Swedish Ken Loach mayhaps? More female lens vibes though.
- This soundtrack is insane and I love it. Blasting *Forever Young* was awesome. Ok... so as I progress through the movie... the soundtrack is sickening, but like intentionally well-done 😭
- Definitely up there with *Requiem For A Dream* and *Trainspotting* for most disgusting cautionary tale films I've ever seen. They really packed a lot in those last 30 minutes.
- Lilya and Volodya's characters are so interesting. They share the same circumstances of being abandoned. They want the same thing, but they go about achieving their goals in two different ways. Volodya wants out, so he wants to jump off the bridge. Lilya wants out, so she's going to hustle her way by any means. Death vs. the grind. One wants to escape life, the other wants to dive head first into it and conquer it.
- It's giving Russian Scarlet Letter 😭
- These actors are insane. Give them all their laurels. It's so naturalistic.
- This movie reminded me of how my mom explained to me the concept of sniffing glue when I was in elementary school.
- Bro... the post-coital McDonald's???? 😭
