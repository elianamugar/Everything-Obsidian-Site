# Thoughts
- Stealing that opening – need to read the shooting script! ![[Gone Girl 1.png]]
	- V.O. over black. The opening image (taken from the middle/end of the film), V.O. continues. Back to black. Establishing shots.
	- **July 5th, The morning of**
- Immediately opens with a juxtaposition between Nick and Amy (starts on a flash-forward with Nick conversing with his sister, Margot)
	- Amy is happy with Nick
	- Nick is unhappy with Amy
	- Five year anniversary
	- Cuts to flashback of Amy writing about how happy she is (diary entry), leading to flashback of their first meeting
- Their first meeting at a party: witty banter, chemistry. Nick is this exciting, adventurous man to Amy. Different than any other man she's known.
- Cuts back to present. Reveals Nick is horrible with gifts. Amy once gifted Nick a notebook so he can go write his novel (implies Nick's a little bit of a failure, not having written his novel).
	- Establishes Amy and Nick as writers, once in New York City. Moved back to **Nick's home** (North Carthage, Missouri) because Nick's mom got sick. She died, but they're still living in Nick's house.
- Flashback: Amy writes another entry of getting proposed to. ***Amazing fucking Amy is getting fucking married.***
	- I noticed that Amy accidentally misstates Sinatra's *New York, New York* lyrics as *These bag of bones shoes* instead of the actual lyrics: *These vagabond shoes*. ([source](https://www.reddit.com/r/MovieDetails/comments/kf2ur0/in_gone_girl_2014_amy_misstates_the_lyrics_when/))
- Cuts back to present. Nick just finished up a polygraph test.
	- Nick reveals Amy has no friends in town.
	- Officer: She sounds like a Type A. You're more laid back. Type B.
	- Officer is shocked Nick hasn't called his wife's parents yet. She commands him to tell them at the payphone.
- Flashback: Amy writes how everyone says marriage is supposed to be hard work, but her and Nick's marriage is *just good*.
	- They accidentally bought each other the same gift.
- Cuts back to present. Nick has to stay at his sister (Go)'s house.
	- Back at the Dunne residence, the officer is approached by a Noelle Hawthorne, who tells the officer that she's Amy's best friend. (contradiction #1: Amy has no friends. Reveals either Nick is lying or doesn't know his wife very well.)
	- Officers find clue \#1
- Next day. **July 6th, One day missing**
	- At police station, in-laws arrived. (Amy's Mother: We knew you should've never moved back here. Nick: We had no other choice.)
	- Press conference. Nick gives a statement – it's very brief and weak. Amy's parents give a statement – long and heartfelt with a call to action.
	- Nick stupidly smiles in front of the Missing Amy poster, while her parents are clearly sad when being taken photos of in front of the Missing Amy poster.
- Later.
	- At the police station, Amy's parents mention an abusive ex as a possible suspect. Nick has no idea about the abusive ex. Reveals Nick doesn't know his wife very well.
	- Nick and Officer find clue \#2. Nick doesn't know how to figure that one out.
- Later that night.
	- Nick goes to his father's house, tripping off an alarm. The police follow him/find him there and suggest it's the answer to clue \#2.
- Flashback: Amy writes in her diary about how their marriage struggles under the recession and the loss of two jobs.
	- Amy's parents got dropped by their publishers, now in debt. They need to dip into her trust fund (exactly $879k).
	- Later. Nick spends money on video games and an Apple laptop. Nick: I don't know how to be without a job.
	- Nick gets a phone call from Go, we don't know what is up, but he's concerned. Amy: And suddenly I knew everything was about to get worse.
- Cuts back to present. **July 7th, Two days missing**
	- Nick shows up to the volunteer alliance for searching for Amy.
	- The officer asks Nick about "Noelle Hawthorne". He doesn't know about her. Suspicion rises for the officers (the male officer thinks Nick is guilty).
	- Shawna Kelly introduces herself to Nick. She gets a selfie with him. Nick stupidly smiles.
	- Marybeth (Amy's mom) is unamused with Nick's stardom.
- Later. The police are at the Dunne residence while the volunteer search team go looking for Amy.
	- Noelle Hawthorne shows up to the door.
- Flashback: Amy writes in her diary *I am a Missourian.*
	- Go told Nick that their mom has stage 4 breast cancer. They moved to Missouri. Amy: I don't mind. I just wished he asked. Nick is happy he's home, but I don't know if he's happy I'm with him.... I feel like I could disappear.
- Cuts back to present.
	- Someone texts Nick *I'm outside.* It's his sidepiece, Andie. Reveals Nick has been cheating on Amy (to the audience). We learn that Nick was working on getting a divorce.
- Flashback: Ay writes in her diary *My husband has come undone*. 
	- Nick's mom died. Amy uses her money on him. She says he just uses her for sex. Amy's proposition: they have a baby. Nick says *Are we really gonna be the couple who has a baby to **save** our marriage?*
	- They fight. Nick pushes Amy against the stairs. Amy: *I didn't scare me that he pushed me. What scared me was that he wanted to hurt me even more.*
- Cuts back to present. Police officers search an abandoned mall.
	- Amy has been there. She wanted a gun, seemed desperate.
- Flashback: Amy writes *For Valentine's Day I thought I should buy a gun. That's how crazy I've become.*
	- Amy says that Nick only sees her as his line of credit. She's paranoid that Nick might hurt her. She says she'd sleep better with a gun.
- Cuts back to present. **July 8th, Three days missing**
	- Go catches Nick escorting Andie out the door. Go: *You're a liar and a cheat, just like dad!* Nick: *Look it wasn't like that. You have no idea how shitty Amy was. How terrible she made me feel.* Paints Nick as a pathetic loser.
	- Nick: *I'm so sick of being picked apart by women.*
- Later. Police station.
	- Images reveal blunt force trauma, blood on floor, she got back up.
- Later that night. Nick goes to Amy's vigil.
	- Some fall in love with Nick. Some think he's creepy.
	- He professes his love for Amy. Andie is in the crowd, mouthing *asshole*.
	- Noelle Hawthorne walks through the crowd, yelling that Amy was six weeks pregnant. Go loses faith in Nick.
	- Nick escapes home. The police follow.
- Later. Dunne residence.
	- Police ask him if he knew Amy was six months pregnant. Nick says Hawthorne's crazy. Police show him photos of how close Hawthorne and Amy were close.
	- Police show Nick how the struggle looked staged. Nick doesn't follow. They lay out to him that he's a prime suspect of homicide.
	- Police reveal Nick is $117k in credit card debt. Nick exclaims he didn't buy any of the stuff. They see Nick really get riled up and yell.
	- Amy's life insurance increased to $1.2mil. Nick says Amy told him to. He filed the paperwork, but she told him to.
- Later. Dunne residence. Go visits Nick.
	- Nick says Amy didn't want kids. Go says that Nick told her that he didn't want kids (Nick rebuts: *I was trying to save face*). At this point, the audience doesn't know who to trust – Amy or Nick.
	- Nick: *I didn't tell you that she didn't want kids because she didn't want me to. Because it would've been another reason for you to hate her. And we had enough of those already! It was easier to just–*
	  Go: *Lie to me?*
	  Nick: *I wanted kids! When we first moved here, I went to a fertility clinic!*
	- Nick has a "little box of hate." A prenup, Amy's ex's letter.
	- Reveal: Nick found clue \#3
- Later. Police at Nick's father's place. Intercut with Nick solving the clue (runs to Go's woodshed full of all the stuff he was accused of buying)
	- Police find Amy's burnt diary but still intact enough so they can read the entries.
- Flashback: **July 5th, The morning of**
	- Amy's POV now: *I am so much happier, now that I'm dead. Technically, "missing".* Amy planned to get Nick in prison for his "murder" knowing that he's cheated and lied to her. She lays out how she executed the entire plan to frame Nick whilst still being alive.
- Cuts back to present. Nick shows Go the woodshed.
	- The morning the film starts is when Nick was gonna divorce Amy, but he couldn't bring himself to do it. Amy tells him "take a walk" knowing he'd go to Sawyer Beach. Go and Nick realize this is Amy's plan: to frame Nick and get him to prison.
	- Go asks a crucial question: *Does Missouri have the death penalty?*
- Flashback: **July 6th, One day gone** - Amy's POV
- Flash-forward: **July 7th, Two days gone** - Amy's POV
- Cuts back to present: **July 9th, Four days gone**
	- Nick visits Tanner Bolt, asking him to be the defense lawyer. The showdown begins to build the better case against Amy. Tanner advises to control public's perception of Amy – build a better narrative.
- Later. Nick visits Tommy O'Hara, one of Amy's exes
	- Nick tells Tommy that Amy said Tommy was physical with her.
	- Tommy says, no, she said he raped her – first degree, felony rape.
		- Amy forced Tommy's hand, Tommy did not fight the case (looking at 30 years of jail). Instead agreed for sexual assault 1, but can't get a job because he has "sexual offender" on every job application.
- Cuts back to Amy (unsure of the time, but it's when everyone found out Amy was supposedly six weeks pregnant).
	- Amy doesn't like that Greta (unknowingly) thinks of her as a spoiled rich girl who married a cheating husband.
- Cuts back to Go and Nick conversing (I think the timelines match up here between Amy and Nick).
	- Nick tells Go about Tanner costing $100k. Go says she will help him. 
	- The news spins the siblings as being incestuously close.
- Cuts back to Amy.
	- The news says Missouri has the death penalty.
	- Amy calls the police towards Go's woodshed.
- Cuts back to Nick visiting Desi Collings.
	- Mr. Collings refuses to talk to Nick.
- Cuts to police station.
	- Rhonda rereads the diary, finding it suspicious that it was being "burnt" in Nick's dad's house. *Why would he burn it at his house?* and *Why didn't he make sure it was completely burnt?*
- Cuts to Go's house.
	- Tanner, Go, and Nick discuss the plan for defense.
- Cuts to Amy. Mini-golf.
	- Amy dropped her fannypack full of money. Greta and the other guy get interested in her money (Great: *I thought you said you didn't come from money?*) – cosplaying poor adds to that spoiled rich girl narrative.
	- Amy goes back to her room. A new sense of paranoia.
- Cuts to Nick. **Six days gone.** Dunne residence.
	- Go brings groceries. Tanner pitches an idea to Nick to go on the news tomorrow. Nick suggests he only needs to reach the heart of *one* viewer (speculating Amy?)
- Cuts to Amy.
	- Greta and Dude ransack her place, looking for the money. They find it and run. Amy screams into a pillow.
	- Amy drives away, calling through pay-phones... but we don't know what she's saying or who she's calling specifically.
- Cuts to Nick prepping for interview.
- Cuts to Amy meeting Desi Collings.
	- She tells him she lost the baby... etc. etc. It's easy for her to build an empathy case with Desi, who's still obsessed with her.
- Cuts to Nick almost going to interview.
- Cuts to Amy watching the news. Andie confesses that she was romantically involved with Nick.
- Cuts to Nick, Tanner, and Go watching the same thing. Nick still decides to go on.
	- Nick looks at a woman, who is visibly disgusted with him.
	- We don't see the interview, but Go and Tanner say he did really good (*Amy brings out the best in me.*)
- Cut to Desi bringing Amy to his bougie lake house.
	- Desi lets Amy know everything is recorded (to comfort her in terms of safety, but I know Amy's gonna use that to her advantage as an escape route just in case).
- Next day. Amy wanders the lake house, scouting for the cameras.
	- Desi comes back. He's very sweet (*A new start*).

- Cut to lake house. Amy and Desi watch the news interview.
	- Now the audience finally sees the interview. Nick admits he was a horrible person, etc. Amy is fixated with the TV, almost excited about the game they're playing.
	- Desi turns off the TV right after the interview.
- Cut to Go's home. Police show up with a search warrant for woodshed (from Amy's phone call).
	- They arrest Go.
- Cut to lake house.
	- Desi takes the tablet away from Amy, who seems fixated on the news. Desi exclaims she kept him dangling for 20 years, then yesterday she chose him. He says he won't force himself on her.

	- The police interview Nick with Amy's burnt diary.

	- Amy continues scheming. She puts a wine glass in her *inner hole* (ew)?? I can't really tell...

	- Amy "escapes" in Desi's car to the driveway. A fairytale story.

	- Rhonda knows Amy's game.

	- They return home. Amy is scary. Nick tells her she can stop pretending.

	- Amy makes breakfast.

	- Nick recites to a mirror about how Amy is a psychotic murderer.
	- Amy prepared him breakfast. They're preparing for an interview.
	- Amy gives a "gift" – she's pregnant. He pushes her against the wall. They argue.

	- The child is his imprisonment. Go: *You want to stay with her*. She can't bare to see him stay with her for 18 years (the age when the kid would technically be an adult).
