# Thoughts
- Learned that this is canonically a prequel to the 82 version! I remember being in school and having watched this, classmates were like "Oh, you watched the original? It's so good!" This, in my head, was the original version, so *of course* I thought.
- This was honestly not bad! I think it gets a lot of hate for the comparison to Carpenter's version, but it's not bad. The effects and style is very 2010s, but the story beats were present and working. The dialogue was a bit off (that guy who just says *Shiiiiit* and *C'mon man let's just go* was pretty funny), but other than that, I thought it was a decent film.
- Tomorrow we will conquer *The Thing From Another World* (1951).
