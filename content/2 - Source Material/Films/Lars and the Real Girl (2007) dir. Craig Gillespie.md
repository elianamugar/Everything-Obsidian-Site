# Thoughts
- Yeah, this is definitely Scout's character.
- This and Punch Drunk Love would be a fantastic double feature.
- The soundtrack reminds me of *Up*.
- Literally needing to aggressively tackle someone in a loving way to get them to come to dinner is so funny.
- Actually thoroughly impressed with this cinematography.
- This movie is so wholesome and sweet :)
- This movie literally made me laugh out loud!
- I think this is my favorite Ryan Gosling performance.
- It feels like everyone kinda fell into the mentality of, welp, if we can't fight him, join him! Plus this whole shebang was doing no harm to anyone.
- I love how protective everyone becomes over Bianca and how everyone loves Lars.
- Lars resuscitating Margo's bear was so sweet.
- Aw, Lars cries when he's happy.
- This is so meta, actors are... acting.
