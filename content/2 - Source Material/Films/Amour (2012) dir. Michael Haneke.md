# Thoughts
- I'm impressed with the quality upload on Internet Archive!
- A slow burner for sure.
- When they're walking together, it also looks like they're dancing together.
- Oh man, the acting! Having older leads with their life experience + acting experience definitely enriches this story.
- A matter of whose sake is she living for. Is he keeping her alive for his own sake? That would be selfish.
- Death is inevitable, that's what makes this film all the more gut-wrenching. It's a matter of how one lives their life, or maybe how much money one decides to throw at it to make the end comfortable. But what makes death comfortable?
- The moments of care feel like watering a plant, even if that plant can no longer survive.
- Haneke makes no effort to hide the end – he reveals the death at the very beginning of the film. Now we just watch the events unfold to the final moments.
- I literally went :O when he slapped her.
