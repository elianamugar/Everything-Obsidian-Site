# Thoughts
- Opening shot. Dead birds falling from the sky. Kirsten Dunst's blue eyes. *Hunters in the Snow* by Pieter Bruegel. Burning down.
- Oh the end of the world never looked more cinematic. Intro reminds me of those  daylight high flash with an underexposed background photography from the 90s fashion magazines.
- This feels like Succession on 2.35 aspect ratio.
- It's sweet, even in such a contained time and place, how Claire (Justine's sister) and Michael (Justine's husband) try their hardest to make Justine happy.
