# thoughts
I had watched this before, but not very thoroughly. It was during Thanksgiving Break during my senior year of college. To be honest, I really watched it for [[Daisy Edgar-Jones]] and no one else really. I was surprised to see [[Harris Dickinson]] in it and he played his role as Chase Andrews well. I think it's funny how these British actors are being imported into American films, but I'm sure doing a southern accent isn't so difficult for them. After all, the southern accent did evolve from the British accent post-colonization. Is the state of American acting in shambles? I personally think British actors are on a much higher level than Americans. We used to have great actors, but now I think that's kind of dwindled, due in part to the quality of material that we have American actors exposed to nowadays.

The general plot of the film is that Kya (played by Daisy Edgar-Jones) grows up being bullied as an estranged "Marsh Girl". This gets her some bad rep as she falls into criminal court, where she's defended by a retired lawyer (can't remember his name). Kya is a genius naturalist who knows the world around her and the world she grew up in. She falls for a guy named Tate who goes to Chapel Hill for college in Biology (or something similar to it) and he doesn't return on July 4th weekend, which leaves Kya in shambles. While Tate is busy trying to graduate, Chase Andrews appears in Kya's life. They romance a bit, but all of a sudden Chase Andrews is engaged without Kya knowing – Kya finds out when she runs into Chase Andrews at the store while she's buying supplies to celebrate her publication of wildlife in her locale.

Overall, I don't have much comments. It was an okay movie, nothing more, nothing less. It got a little bit boring in the flashbacks as I was only interested in the modern story, but I liked how they tried to mix up the chronology throughout the film to make it less boring. They just could've shortened the flashback beats a little bit. The acting was pretty good! I didn't expect any less. I just needed to watch this because I'm looking for some inspiration regarding [[Lost & Found]]. I heard there was a love triangle in this, but it wasn't quite what I was looking for. Unfortunately, there was one love interest that was more unlikeable than the other, but that's not what I'm trying to do.
