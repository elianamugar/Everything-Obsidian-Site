# Thoughts
- Did not remember Bryan Cranston being in this. Wait the cast is insane. Oscar Isaac?
- Produced by Marc Platt? Wtf.
- The original *Baby Driver* (sans music) and prequel to *Fall Guy*.
- A great quick opening sequence.
- It's insane how those action scenes hit.
- The final third falls apart for me. It's giving very much a man wrote this because how does it make any sense that a woman's neighbor confesses that her husband was involved with some bad guys and he tried to help the husband, then witnessed her husband get killed, then kisses her in the elevator?
	- Oh, your husband got killed? And I just told you about it? Now I'm gonna kiss you in the elevator. Like BRUH. And then, I'm gonna kill a man in the elevator!
- I also think there wasn't enough to realistically develop and believe Shannon and Driver's father/son relationship. Now that I think about it, there wasn't even time to realistically develop the relationship between Driver and Irene either.
- The cine is really cool though.
- Lots of slow-mos.
- I have mixed feelings on the edit. Some things really worked, and some could've been tighter/less flashy. Not sure how I feel about those fade sequences.
- Sound was great.
