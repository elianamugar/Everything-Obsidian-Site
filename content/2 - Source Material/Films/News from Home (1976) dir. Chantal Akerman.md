# Thoughts
- Establishing quiet shots of New York, followed by voiceover of her mother's letters
- Interesting about the title "News from Home" - for me it has two meanings:
	1) The news source/subject is from home (France), or
	2) Receiving the news from home (New York)
- I watched this because 1) it was on my syllabus for *Vagabonds* and 2) I wanted to watch *Jeanne Dielman, 23, quai du Commerce, 1080 Bruxelles*, but it has a 3h22m runtime and... It's already 5:30pm. I'm gonna have to start earlier in the day for that one.
- Her mother reminds me of Mom, in the way that she'll send care packages and send messages, even if there's no response. The earnestness of wanting to help her daughter and the intimacy between mother and daughter, knowing how the daughter reacts... it pulls at the heartstrings.
	- "I know sunny weather depresses you, and you don't even have sandals."
- The mother urges her to write more to them.
- It's oddly hypnotic, this film. But honestly, I'm getting hungry... (paused with 1h15m left).
- This is honestly not my vibe... it's very French. A much more quiet Varda documentary style.
- My favorite shot is in the subway, where this gentleman around his 50s wears a striking light-green outfit with an iconic plaid pants. He just stares at the camera for the longest time, then escapes to a different subway cart. Love him. Thinking of him.
- Her mother's taste in things is like me:
	- "I received your screenplay. It's well-written, but you know my taste: I find it sad and gloomy. Those people sure have a hard life. It's an important social issue. I hope it will turn out well. The public must be made aware of all this suffering that you young people see so clearly."
- The letters reveal the gradual decay of her family at home in a bittersweet way. It reminds me of this Ocean Vuong poem of her mother's Amazon cart receipts overtime, which reveals her mother's cancer and eventual death.
