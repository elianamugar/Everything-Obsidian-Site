# Thoughts
- Visiting Alcatraz helped me digest the prison part.
- Main actor kinda looks like blue-eyed Evan Peters + Harry Styles.
- Focuses on the *extremes* of systems. Starts with the over-indulgence of the self, then shifts to the extreme of removing the complete self. (The priest makes a good point here – he's not making the moral choice to do good, he's avoiding doing bad stuff out of fear.)
- A very interesting watching experience... especially since I haven't read the book and only know very general themes of the book. Definitely a critique of the extremes of systems. We're a product, subject to our systems, the pawns of the board. Not my vibe of adaptation style, but a decent go of it.
- Leads to guilt and shame.
