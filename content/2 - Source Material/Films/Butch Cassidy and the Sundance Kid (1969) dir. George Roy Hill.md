# Thoughts
- Interesting sepia film for the intro, then to normal film color.
- The landscape shots are awesome.
- The cine is incredible.
- The first half was kinda boring, with some gag bits scattered throughout it. They just wanna enlist and fight the Spanish! C'mon!
- Around halfway, I'm enjoying their dynamic.
- Whether they know it or not... what an interesting polyamorous situation "Butch" and "Sundance" have found themselves in!
- I sometimes like the music they play. My favorite one is right after they rob the Spaniards. ("South American Getaway" - Burt Bacharach)
- Lots of robbery montage.
- RIP Robert Redford. He was awesome in this (also Paul Newman).
