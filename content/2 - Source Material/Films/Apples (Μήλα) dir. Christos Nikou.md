# Thoughts
This reminded me a lot of *Perfect Days* by Wim Wenders, where it's mostly a guy doing things, but in this case he's trying to get his identity (a new one or get his old identity back, that's debatable). The cinematography in this was beautiful – I absolutely loved the color (the blues are to die for). This might be due to the film stock they used, but either way, it was beautiful in its 4:3 goodness. The soundtrack in this was phenomenal – fantastic choice of music. The editing was great too!
