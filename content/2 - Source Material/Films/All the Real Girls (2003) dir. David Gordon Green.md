# Thoughts
- This is very small town drama vibes. Didn't expect there to be an awesome scene of clowns dancing in this *at all*. 
- Whoever plays Uncle Leland's kid deserve all their acting laurels. But why did they name her character Feng-Shui ;-;
- I hate the CONSTANT soundtrack.
- The sequences are kinda confusing? I was not getting the time jumping back and forth because they wouldn't really indicate it, plus the characters barely change in age so that makes it more difficult.
- Not loving the dialogue -- highlights trauma-bonding as being intimate... It's giving *you're not like other girls.* And *this is different*. 
- Oh god, not the manic pixie girl portrayal.
- The further into the movie, the more apparent it is how atrocious the writing, dialogue, and overall script is. This is really being carried by the actors working with what they've got.
- The editing is really janky and the tone is inconsistent... like why in the last third of the film have you now included time lapse sequences???
- This movie really did not age well. It's very much life through the male lens.
- I'm impressed with how Paul Schneider and Patricia Clarkson acted through that final clown scene cuz... it was really hard for me to take seriously.
- A great example of what not to do for Vagabonds.
