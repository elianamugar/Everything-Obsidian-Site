# Thoughts
This is about Jesse James, a real assassin that was a part of the James Gang, known for robbing trains and banks. They were a group of Southern loyalists. Roger Deakins was the cinematographer for this film (it looks absolutely amazing). I didn't realize that this was based on the real people – I mean I'd heard of Jesse James before, but I didn't connect the dots. The premise is interesting – it's Robert Ford (played by Casey Affleck) shadowing the master Jesse James (played by Brad Pitt). Bob Ford is portrayed as this chum (or "nincompoop" as he says) that's a wannabe great. I really wanted to like this movie, but the pacing in the middle was just SOOO LONG. The ending pace was not my vibe, but I really did love the last two frames of the film.
