# Thoughts
- The soundtrack!
- The physicality of the bodies... the dancing and aggressiveness is such a delicate mix of rigidity and fluidity. They're like dancers more than soldiers!
- The tightness of the film is impressive.
- Emphasis on showing to evoke.
- Loosely based on Herman Melville's *Billy Budd* and used parts of the *Billy Budd* opera in the soundtrack.
- The Kuleshov effect in full motion.
- The TCM intro set up great context going into this film.
