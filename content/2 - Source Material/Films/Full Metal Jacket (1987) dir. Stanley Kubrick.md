# Thoughts
- Almost through with the Kubrick discography! I've been looking forward to this one. Still need to cover *Eyes Wide Shut* and *Paths of Glory*. *Barry Lyndon* is still my fave Kubrick.
- Vincent D'Onofrio!! Private Pyle is a little cutie patootie. Love the relationship between Pyle and Joker.
- Depiction is not romanticization. I take this quote with me to everything I watch.
- Effective and efficient use of montages in the beginning for the drill training.
- Yeah, not everything aged well, but again... depiction is not romanticization.
- I love that scene when, in the middle of the night, the Marines beat Pyle. It mimics sneaking around in the forest in Vietnam War.
- Kubrick stare with Pyle.
- The character focus and character building is so good in this. A model in doing so.
- *These Boots Were Made For Walkin'* - Nancy Sinatra
- Oop Pyle said the title!
- *Bird is the word*.
- The acting in that last scene is phenomenal.
