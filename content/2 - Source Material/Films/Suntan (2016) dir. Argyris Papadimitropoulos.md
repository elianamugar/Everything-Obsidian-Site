# Thoughts
This film was very Lolita-esque. The character of Kostis (Κοστις) was a loveable innocent guy, but becomes unlikeable towards the end of the film. It doesn't take the story of Lolita the way Nabokov intended – the director of this film made the character we follow likable, but he was unable to portray Kostis's motivations as the truth. I think that was the key to Nabokov's Lolita – Nabokov was able to make the audience believe the truth of the character we follow, which ultimately should make the readers disgusted by the entire thought process of the protagonist. Instead, that completely shatters once Kostis's incel-ness comes to light. It was an okay film, but not a big fan.
