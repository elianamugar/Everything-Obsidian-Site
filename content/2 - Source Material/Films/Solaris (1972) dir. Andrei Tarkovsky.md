# Thoughts
- Leaves you with more questions than answers, but not in a good way. Shot beautifully with a wonderful set design though, but I have so many gripes with this film that I can't even waste the energy to type them out. I respect Tarkovsky's craft, I really do. But his work doesn't seem to be for me.
- The first act was not necessary at all.
- Takes up time, not in a good way. Many scenes could be taken away/cut short. Dialogue could be cleaned up.
- The character work is horrendous. Kris's decisions feel so unhuman and genuinely don't make sense for his character.
- I understand what Tarkovsky was going for, but its execution was not made for these times maybe. Maybe it really connected for the 70s.
- The philosophy is not hard to understand, but it's hidden under the guise of being deep and symbolic and complex. It's not.
- I guess he's big on contemplating nature and I'm more of a character person.
- This could've benefitted from *a lot*... but a main one is a soundtrack.
- Good last shot. But that's all I'll give it.
- Under the illusion of *showing more than telling*, but in actuality, Tarkovsky overuses the tool of dialogue to explain the philosophy that he's *trying* to show us in the film. Lots of that could've been cut, especially in the library scene where it felt like I was watching a talking play rather than watching a film unfold with symbolic metaphors. 
- The dwarf had me gagged.
