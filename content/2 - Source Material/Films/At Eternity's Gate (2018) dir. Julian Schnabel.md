# Thoughts
Wow. I didn't expect to be so taken with the visuals of this film. What a surprise. I liked this film a lot more than I thought. I did chuckle when I saw that meme photo that looks like this:
![[At Eternity's Gate 1.png]]
I'm pretty proud of my review on Letterboxd. It's an account of my genuine impression of the film while watching it:
>"Throughout the ENTIRE film, the cinematography and camerawork was so visually striking right when that first frame shows up after Willem Dafoe's opening lines over black, it gave me that same indescribably whirlwind feeling of jumping into film that I felt when I first watched <i>The Celebration</i>. And the editing choices? Awesome. This is a film that's so sure of itself and knows its identity. It's crazy how much this reminds me of <i>The Revenant</i> and <i>The Lighthouse</i> – it's claustrophobically compelling while at the same time so freeing with its form. Idk how else to describe this film – it's just a film that makes <i>sense</i>. The visual language manages to show the push and pull Van Gogh grappled with the society that rejected him and the muses that embraced him. Yeah, this captures the spirit and essence of Van Gogh and his contemporaries. It feels very... humanist? My only gripes would be with some of the dialogue or the direction of the performances, but other than that, this was a great experience."
