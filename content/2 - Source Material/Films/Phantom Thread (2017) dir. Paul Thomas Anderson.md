# Thoughts
- I kept seeing clips of PTA testing film stock for this film and that warranted this rewatch.
- The score is captivating, the pace is perfect, and the acting is just phenomenal.
- Why did I rate it 3.5 stars initially? The world may never know. The first time I watched it was in high school, so maybe I was a bit jaded by historical dramas at the time?
- The dress measurement scene actually made me laugh out loud.
- I'm stupidly like :) the whole time.
- Keep your enemies close, but keep your muses closer (smirk emoji).
- It goes without saying, but the costume design is perfect.
