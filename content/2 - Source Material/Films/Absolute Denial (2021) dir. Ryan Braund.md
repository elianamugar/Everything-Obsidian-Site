# Thoughts
- Fantastic film with a 70ish minute runtime for big nerds about computers (me). But also a great film in general, tbh. It breaks down the way computers, programming, and AI works in a digestible way with cool animation!
- One question: Why didn't he buy a warehouse first, then ship all of his equipment there instead of shipping it to his dorm room, then driving back and forth to the warehouse?? You'd think a computer programmer who's trained to think in efficiency, he would've figured out any basic pathfinding algorithm in his head before executing this lol.
- How was he able to afford all that equipment??
- I'm assuming all of this was rotoscoped - meaning all of this was practically filmed, then drawn over?
- I didn't really get the ending... but I guess the audience isn't able to tell if he's in a simulation or not.
