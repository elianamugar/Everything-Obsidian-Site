# Quotes
- Taken from Eva Victor's Sundance interview: *Sorry, Baby* is a story for those who want to see that it's actually possible for a person to emerge from trauma and someday want to be alive again, to someday unfreeze enough to feel optimistic about things ahead. Well, maybe not optimistic, that's a stretch. But at least curious about the possibility of stuff happening at some point in their lives that isn't horrible. This film is for those who survive every day, quietly.

- It's just like that sometimes. I'm sorry that bad things are going to happen to you. I hope they don't. But sometimes bad stuff just happens. That's why I feel bad for you in a way. That you're alive and you don't know that yet.

# Thoughts
- This soundtrack is ethereal. That opening score just blew me away.
- Static shots.
- That house shot + long driving scene is absolutely haunting.
- The comedic undertones really sell the story.
- Fuck... perfectly executed the *MC finds solace in a stray pet* storyline that I'm using in *Vagabonds*!
- John Carroll Lynch's short appearance in his scene made me tear up.
- Some of the acting was weird for me, but it's alright.
