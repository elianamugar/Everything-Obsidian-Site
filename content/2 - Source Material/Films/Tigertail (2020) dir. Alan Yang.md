# Thoughts
- Opens on a VO over black screen. Cut to grass blowing in the wind.
- The soundtrack is amazing! But sometimes... there's music where there shouldn't be. Where silence would suffice (actually, I'd go on to see *needed* to emphasize certain points).
- Love the play between Taiwanese and Mandarin.
- Dialogue feels too on-the-nose for me. Or, I guess, it's too... stiff? I can point out the dialogue that I would strip away, where silence would suffice and communicate the words on screen. Lots of hand-holding through the dialogue, which I don't like.
- Tries too hard to be melodramatic.
- Great cinematography.
