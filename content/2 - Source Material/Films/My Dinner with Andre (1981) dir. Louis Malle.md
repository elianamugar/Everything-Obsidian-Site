# Thoughts
- https://www.reddit.com/r/Letterboxd/comments/1m3jhae/need_to_find_this_movie/ - this is what led me to watch the movie (finally).
- Andre sounds relatable.
- Shoutout to the only Bergman I've watched (*Autumn Sonata*)
- Whoever says VO is too much – I'm gonna show them this movie.
- I can't believe I'm watching a conversation between two guys for about 2 hours.
- Great dialogue, but it feels like I'm witnessing the screenwriter's outlet to vent... feels like a lecture. Don't get me wrong, it had my attention, but still very much like an engrossing podcast.
- A little bit on the side of pretentious.
- The ending music on Erik Satie's *Gymnopédie No. 1* was kinda chef's kiss.
