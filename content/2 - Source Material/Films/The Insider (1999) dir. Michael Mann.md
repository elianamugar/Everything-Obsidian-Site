# Thoughts
- A fantastic thriller-esque *Erin Brokovich* which on the surface advertises itself as a whistleblower story against Big Tobacco, but truly covers the corporate workplace politics of journalism – the conflict of private vs. public interest and, most of all, how much does keeping a promise matter?
- Interesting how Mann made this into a thriller. I'd directly compare this to *Erin Brokovich* and the difference in how this was executed versus that one.
- Highkey the entire cast's performances are fantastic, but Al Pacino's really blew me away and surprised me with how much his presence takes over the screen because I'm aware they really pushed for Russell Crowe to be the spotlit star for this film.
- Around the 3/4 mark, the film started to really drag and it got tiring just following the story around, but other than that, everything else was fantastic.
- Oh, and the cine is amazing.
