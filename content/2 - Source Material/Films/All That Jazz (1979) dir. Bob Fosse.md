# Thoughts
- Awesome opening.
- Big fan of the editing.
- Man, this performance of dancing and acting is so impressive.
- The repetitive editing of taking the pills and the morning routine is a good inspo for Vagabonds.
- The sound design's incredible. The scene when they're doing the script reading and all we hear are Joe's sounds.
- John Lithgow appearance!!
- This musical movie takes full advantage of the movie format (as seen in the manipulation of the editing). 
- I have mixed feelings on the last third. It was exhilarating, thrilling, and confusing in the best way. But the tone didn't really fit in with the rest of the film... I guess it really is *all that jazz*. And maybe it dragged on for me.
