# Thoughts
The production design and color of this entire film was amazing! I was not a big fan of the musical though, I didn't expect the **entire** film to be sung. I mainly watched this because it was one of Chazelle's main inspiration for *La La Land (2016)*, so I thought I should watch one of the source materials! Needless to say, it didn't really hit. I like what Chazelle drew from the film into *La La Land*, but I don't think I'll be using this as inspiration anytime soon.
