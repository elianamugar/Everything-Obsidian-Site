# Thoughts
I've already written one too many essays about this book, so I'll keep the movie thoughts short (as I've already watched this many times before). I feel like this film really captures the essence of glitz and glamor and vanity that F. Scott Fitzgerald was really trying to depict in his novel. Honestly, I was inspired to watch this because I kept seeing TikToks of *party 4 u* by Charlie XCX edited to this film – and this story is the epitome of the song! I don't think it has anything that can directly inspire *Lost & Found*, but it was a good love triangle watch. I love the music in this and I was surprised to see Elizabeth Debicki (who portrayed *Princess Diana* in *The Crown (S5-6)*).
