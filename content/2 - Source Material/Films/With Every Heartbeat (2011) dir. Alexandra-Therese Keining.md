# Thoughts
I was SOOO disappointed in this film! Let me first off say this is originally called *Kyss mig* in the originally released Swedish. The reason for my disappointment is it somewhat executed the idea I had for [[Lost & Found]] poorly! There was no build up between Mia and Frida and they just *happened* to be passionately in love all of a sudden! Mia is engaged to man she loves (they've been dating for about 7 years), so yeah, it was hard to buy that she fell for her *future sister-in-law* after immediately hating her at the family get together and, then seeing her at an isolated cabin retreat with the family. Also, Mia's character is supposed to be Type-A, so it's even MORE unbelievable!! If it's hard for me to buy in, it's hard for me to buy out when the ending hits. Literally all the elements of *the main character has an existential crisis between choosing her fiancé (soon to be spouse) and her other love interest that's closely related to the fiancé* were set up in this film, but it missed the mark. I'm also surprised Tim (Mia's fiancé) just gave up so easily. Maybe he didn't "give up" but for lack of better words and lack of understanding anything else that was portrayed on the screen, he gave up. I think the acting carried this, but other than that it was a very *meh* film.
