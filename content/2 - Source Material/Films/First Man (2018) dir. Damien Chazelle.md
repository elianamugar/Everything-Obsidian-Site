# Thoughts
- Theme of funerals is apparent. Neil Armstrong attends, firstly, his daughter (Karen)'s funeral and hosts the post-funeral gathering at his house. He walks through the hallways, empty. He hides in his room, crying and closing the notebooks full of recorded notes of Karen's treatments. Then Elliott, his friend and fellow Gemini astronaut, dies in a T-38 crash. He walks through the hallways of Elliott's house (post-funeral gathering as well), empty and realizing the weight of mortality. Four pilots also died during their time at Edwards AFB ("We got really good at funerals that year" -Janet)
- On rewatch, I think this is my favorite biopic, hands down. It's just really good and sorely underrated. How unfortunate (but fortunate for me!)
- Apollo 1 failure kills 3 astronauts in a plugs-out test/launch rehearsal test. Electrical wiring issue causes fire in cabin. Comms issue, can't hear the astronauts. "We're burning up in here!" Emergency hatch doesn't work (why?)
	- From the wiki: "The ignition source of the fire was determined to be electrical, and the fire spread rapidly due to combustible nylon material and the high-pressure pure oxygen cabin atmosphere. Rescue was prevented by the [plug door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plug_door "Plug door") hatch, which could not be opened against the internal pressure of the cabin. Because the rocket was unfueled, the test had not been considered hazardous, and emergency preparedness for it was poor."
	- Execution of scene: Cutting back and forth between Neil at White House, schmoozing guests to campaign and keep them happy about spending taxpayer money on NASA (even though there are other problems the money could go towards) and astronauts in cockpit. Lots of steady tension, lots of silence, but short scenes in the cockpit. The calm before the storm vibes. Then chaos ramps up in what seems like less than minute. Then an explosion from outside the cockpit. Later on, scenes show the leftover of the explosion in the cockpit.
- The sound design is FANTASTIC. I didn't notice it before, but it really makes this film.
- The music theme ties in very nicely in the film.
- This is so underrated in Chazelle's filmography.
- Aspect ratio change!
