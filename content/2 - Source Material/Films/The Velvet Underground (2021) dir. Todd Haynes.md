I am anonymous and have forgotten myself. It is always so when one goes to the movies. It is, as they say, a drug.

# Thoughts
- I don't like Lou Reed staring at me throughout this doc.
- Was in the mood for a music documentary, so I put this on.
- Definitely an experimental doc style by Todd Haynes himself (reminds me of his earlier work *Superstar: The Karen Carpenter Story*). Going into this without knowing anything about The Velvet Underground (besides the name Lou Reed), it felt like I was an outsider to this doc. It wasn't very invitational, but instead, very intimidating. Interesting style though.
- The style of the doc didn't really grab me at the beginning, but it grew on me throughout the middle part.
- Overall, the doc won me over. Super interesting and worthwhile.
