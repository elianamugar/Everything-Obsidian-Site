# Thoughts
- Another Cillian Murphy and Emily Watson team-up! (re: *Small Things Like These*)
- I'm a sucker for teacher movies, so very much looking forward to this one.
- There is a George McKay doppelganger in this.
- Phenomenal breakout performances.
- The entire film is beautiful and heartbreaking at the same time and definitely sheds a light on the humanity of it all, even when the situation looks inhumane (and animalistic at times) on the outside. That ending was killer – a gut-wrenching heartfelt note. It reminds me of that line in *Lady Bird*, when the nun asks her,"Don't you think maybe they are the same thing — love and attention?" despite seeming to hate.
