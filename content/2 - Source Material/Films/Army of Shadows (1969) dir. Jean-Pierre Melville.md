# Thoughts
- The cinematography is incredible.
- The soundtrack is incredible.
- In love with this film stock. Was it the same from Le Samouraï?
	- Oh, this is the same director of both films.
- Fantastic costumes.
- I need Madame Mathilde's shoes.
- I'm really obsessed with the poster.
- Impressive editing.
- A decent WW2 flick focusing on the perspective of the French resistance.
- The story can feel a bit stale at times, but it's got that Jean-Pierre Melville kick to it.
