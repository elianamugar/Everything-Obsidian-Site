# Thoughts
- Cool color shift.
- Editing is crazy. Experimental, for sure.
- Sound is shitty, but I think this is when they had to overdub in booth.
- This is the Manila and the Philippines I've seen as an outsider and heard through stories passed down from generations in my family. There's some small scenes next to a prominent river and I was in awe in how clean it is. I've only ever grown up knowing that the river has been polluted, its water levels low, but still enough water for those living near it to wash their clothes in the dirty water. I've seen people scraping for trash to recycle, while stepping on small islands of pollution on that river. Seeing it so clean in that short scene just boggled my mind, truly.
- Warning: Not everything is English subtitled. Some will flash on the screen, but lots of sentences go on untranslated it. A good thing to keep in mind if planning to watch on the Internet Archive.
- Maybe the moral of the story is... in the system of capitalism, you're selling your body regardless unless you're at the tippy-top of the pyramid!
- You don't get to see many Filipino films like this.
- Great social commentary.
- Maybe the plot gets a muddled. It seems like it's trying to cover a lot of ground in a short amount of time (social commentary, romance, sex work, material conditions, etc.)
- The ending dragged for me. Messy third act.
