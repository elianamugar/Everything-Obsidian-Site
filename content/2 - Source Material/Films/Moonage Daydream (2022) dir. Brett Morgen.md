# Thoughts
- God, I started these notes too late. I was just watching this to see if I was interested in buying it from the Criterion sale that B&N has, but I see it's a mistake to note take notes.
- Bowie mentions he feels like an "empty vessel" when he's not writing or performing, and it's so interesting that I hear this word amongst creatives and artists. They feel like a vessel that needs to be filled, whether by God, some spiritual energy, or other people. For example, Charlotte Ritchie doesn't feel she can produce work herself, but rather is a vessel to be acted through and to bring other works to life. Jon Bellion also feels like a vessel to create music from God and when the magic isn't there, he doesn't force himself to make music.
- I highly relate to Bowie. He identifies as a generalist. I do too.
- An optimistic version of Scout.
