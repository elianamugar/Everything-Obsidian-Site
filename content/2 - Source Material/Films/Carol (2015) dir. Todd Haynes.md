# Thoughts
I thought it was a decent film! The middle-end of the film kind of dragged for me, unfortunately, but other than that, it was pretty solid. I was looking for some inspiration in this, but I didn't quite find it. The love triangle felt like your typical period love triangle, mainly due to restrictions of the law in the time period. *Lost & Found* will be set in the contemporary with the more relaxed laws and acceptable culture around these topics, so... I'm stumped there. Cate Blanchett's American accent is literally so good in this.
