# thoughts
I thought the film was exactly what it promised – a retelling of the 1970 Miss World mishap. I honestly watched it for Emma Corrin, but the cast itself was super stacked. I'll mainly relay my review that I posted on Letterboxd.

I appreciated the film's depiction of the nuanced stakes for different women, prioritizing *showing* rather than *telling* (and other subtextual tools) for the women facing intersectionality. One specific editing cut I enjoyed was the mention of "cattle auction" in the TV debate, which was immediately followed by the scene of Eric Morley telling the assistants to "Herd them! Herd them!" I thought that was pretty cheeky and clever. The dialogue was a bit too cheesy and on the nose for me, but at the same time, I believe that's how these women would've been having these conversations with each other.

Overall, I didn't enjoy the editing of this film and the film itself was meh.
