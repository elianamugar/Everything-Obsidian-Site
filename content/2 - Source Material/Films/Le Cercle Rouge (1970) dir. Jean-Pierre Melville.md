# Thoughts
- Melville loves to put Alain Delon in a trench coat.
- What the fuck are up with those spiders.
- The dance club scenes are fun. Added bonus that the club is called *Sartre's*. Very fitting.
- I was actually really excited to watch this Melville – it's the one I was most looking forward to out of the two I've already watched (*Army of Shadows*, *Le Samouraï*) simply based on the premise, but it turned out to be my least favorite so far. The pacing was really not for me and the character work was a lot less established. I was not as pulled into the story to care about it. The actual heist scenes were fun. Maybe I'll give *Rififi* a try and maybe I'll like that one better.
