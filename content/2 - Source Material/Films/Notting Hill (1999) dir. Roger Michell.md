# Thoughts
I've had a few ideas clinking around for a celebrity romance story – one in prose format and the other in screenplay format (re: [[Lost & Found]]). I think this is more true to the prose format than Lost & Found, but it still gives some insight into how I'm thinking of writing [[Aubrey]]'s character. This film reassured me that I didn't have to think too much about the paparazzi-celebrity dynamics – there were some moments where Anna Scott was able to just walk around Notting Hill, and since this takes place in the late 90s, I thought that paparazzi bombardment must've died down a little since then! The middle of the film was a bit boring for me, but it was insightful to see how William Thacker dealt with Anna Scott's celebrity status (re: [[Jackie]]). 

I really liked Thacker's quote:
>Of course, I've seen her films and always thought she was, well, fabulous -- but, you know, million miles from the world I live in. Which is here -- Notting Hill -- not a bad place to be...

And Scott's quote:
>I'm just also just a girl, standing in front of a boy, asking him to love her.
