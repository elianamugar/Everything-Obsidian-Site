# thoughts
I watched this for some [[Lost & Found]] inspiration, considering that there was a love triangle. I will admit, it was somewhat closer to what I was intending with regards to the screenplay, so I will be heavily taking this film into consideration. I was inspired to watch this because I heard the [[Black Mirror]] episode *Hotel Reverie* was heavily inspired by this.

The general plot is that Laura Jesson (played by Celia Johnson) slowly falls in love with her train companion Alec Harvey (played by Trevor Howard). Laura describes the general course of her relationship with Alec to her husband as a "dream".

The part that worked really well for me was the casual friendship that develops during their visits into something more emotionally fulfilling than either expected (ripped from Letterboxd's synopsis). This is what I'm trying to capture in Lost & Found. I absolutely adored the ending of the film, where they have to part ways because Alec is going to South Africa to work at his brother's practice and Laura is so overcome by her emotions that she *almost* walks onto the train tracks. But she doesn't. The way that part was shot was wonderful! I'd steal that in a heartbeat.

I'm also pretty sure [[Todd Haynes]] stole the hand-shoulder touch scene in this film for [[Carol (2015) dir. Todd Haynes]]. In *Carol*, Haynes starts with the ending, much like this film does. And the added restraint of not being able to part a proper goodbye through a silent hand-shoulder touch is iconic.

One of my favorite takeaway quotes from this movie is from Laura Jesson: *Oh Fred, I've been so foolish. I've fallen in love. I'm an ordinary woman. I didn't think such violent things could happen to ordinary people.*
