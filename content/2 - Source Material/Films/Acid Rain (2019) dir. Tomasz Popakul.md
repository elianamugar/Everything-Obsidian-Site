# Thoughts
- I tried starting this before but the trippy visuals actually really freaked me out so I stopped watching. But now I'm here to conquer this short film once and for all (before watching *The Thing* (2011) with Ben and Caleb). 
- Animation freaks me out, but it's also good with a unique style. It's gritty and cool.
- Soundtrack makes me feel uneasy af (last time I felt like this was when watching *Punch-Drunk Love*).
- I feel like I'm in a PS2 game.
- I had to rewind to see if the guy actually just walked through the table. He did. That's alright though, just thought it was funny.
- Midway through - definitely more on visuals than story. Nothing against it, just purely an observation.
- An uncomfortable watch (intentionally so).
- The final act is horrifying. Definitely escalated a little too fast, but horrifying nonetheless.
- The pacing of the end was actually a bit too fast (missing some beats I guess). Didn't feel like the two characters built up an emotional attachment for the audience to really care about the ending.
