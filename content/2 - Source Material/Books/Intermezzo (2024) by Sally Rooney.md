# Thoughts
I definitely feeling Sally Rooney's Marxist views in this quite strongly, especially with the conversations between Ivan and Margaret, and also the conversations Ivan has in his head about his observations with people (especially of Peter). A very interesting observation in how grief manifests in people.

Man, that scene towards the end where Peter goes to watch Ivan's game and accidentally says "Our dad should be here," then corrects himself in front of Margaret to say, "Sorry, our dad would've been here" was a quiet sort of heartbreak. But the part that was even more heartbreaking was when Margaret was going to see Ivan after the game and Peter said he shouldn't go since Ivan would probably still me upset at him and that Peter just wanted to be there. Aw, my poor babies.
