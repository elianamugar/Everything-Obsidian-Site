And if that means the human species is going to die out, isn’t in a way a nice reason to die out, the nicest reason you can imagine? Because when we should have been reorganizing the distribution of the world’s resources and transitioning collectively to a sustainable economic model, we were worrying about sex and friendship instead. And I love that about humanity, and in fact it’s the very reason I root for us to survive – because we are so stupid about each other.
