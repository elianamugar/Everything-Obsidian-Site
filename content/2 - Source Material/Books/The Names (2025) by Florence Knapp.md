# Thoughts
I was scared of reading this book the moment I realized that this was going to follow three different timelines. I have *Midnight Library* to thank for that. This book interested me because I am always curious about the naming of things, which I guess falls under my love of linguistics and etymology. Why do things mean what they mean? Is it the cause or the effect that came first? The ultimate question of this book is "Can a name change the course of a life?" It's interesting what the Book of the Month polls readers for this book: "Do you think your name affects your future?" I do believe these are slightly two different questions – one asking "does your name determine your future" and the other being "does it affect your life, but your future is already written out for you?" I think Knapp does a great job navigating this question without offering an outright answer. I love that each character's name had a reason behind it and, at the end of the book, Knapp provides a character name sheet which defines each of their meanings. I put 5/5 on Goodreads for this book, but if I could do half stars, I'd lean more towards 4.5/5 simply because I *absolutely hate* when a book writes about COVID. I think Knapp does a great job at navigating the circumstance of COVID as it was unavoidable regarding the plot device she used, but nevertheless I still hate it. But other than that, I think she managed to navigate the three different possible lives that could've been lived very well. It's what *Midnight Library* thought it was.
