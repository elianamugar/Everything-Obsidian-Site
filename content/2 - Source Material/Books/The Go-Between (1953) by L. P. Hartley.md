The room faced the street and was a little stuffy, for we used it seldom and when it was not in use the windows were fastened to keep out the dusty, which in the dry weather rose in clouds whenever a vehicle went by. It was our one formal room and I think my mother may have chosen it for its moral effect; its comparative strangeness would be a step towards the strangeness I should feel in another house.
