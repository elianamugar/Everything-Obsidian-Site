Month 1: Focus on alphabet, key phrases, polite interactions
- Week 1-2:
	- Master the alphabet (write & read signs, menus)
	- Learn 50+ essential phrases (e.g. "Πού είναι η τουαλέτα" – _Where is the bathroom?_)
	- Numbers 1-100 (prices, time)
	- Practice pronunciation
- Week 3-4:
	- Food/drink vocabulary (menus, markets)
	- Directions + transport phrases (e.g. "Πόσο κοστίζει" – _How much does it cost?_)
	- Simple present tense (e.g. "Θέλω..." - *I want...*)
	Tools:
	- Language Transfer (first 10 lessons)
	- Duolingo (travel sections)
	- Memrise ("Greek for Travel" course)

Month 2: Focus on deeper interactions + listening comprehension
- Week 5-6:
	- Hotel/Airbnb check-in phrases
	- Emergency phrases (e.g. "Βοήθεια!" – *Help!*)
	- Past tense
- Week 7-8:
	- Market haggling (e.g. "Είναι λίγο ακριβό" – _It’s a bit expensive_)
	- Asking for recommendations (e.g. "Τι προτείνετε;" – _What do you recommend?_)
	- Listening practice (Greek music, **Slow Greek** podcast)
- Tools:
	- Tandom/HelloTalk/AI (role-play travel scenarios)
	- Easy Greek YouTube (watch with subs)

Month 3: Focus on fluency in travel contexts + cultural awareness
- Week 9-10:
	- Small talk (weather, hobbies)
	- Slang + gestures (e.g. "Εντάξει" – _OK_, nodding ≠ "no")
	- Mock conversations (pretend you're at a taverna)
- Week 11-12:
	- Watch Greek travel vlogs (pick up accents)
	- Learn regional quirks (e.g. Cretan dialect differences)
	- Review weak spots (ask natives on Tandem or AI)
- Tools:
	- Clozemaster (travel-related sentences)
	- GreekPod101 ("Survival Greek" series)

Travel-specific:
- Prioritize
	- "Μιλάτε Αγγλικά;" (_Do you speak English?_)
	- "Ένα εισιτήριο, παρακαλώ" (_One ticket, please_).
	- "Η λογαριασμός, παρακαλώ" (_The bill, please_).
	- "Πόσο κάνει;" (_How much is it?_).
- Cultural tips
	- Say **"γεια σας" (hello)** when entering shops.
	- Greeks often talk with hands—observe gestures!
	- Dinner is late (9-11pm)—adjust expectations!
- Emergency prep
	- Save these numbers: **112 (EU emergency), 100 (police).**
	- Learn: "Χρειάζομαι γιατρό" (_I need a doctor_).
- Carry a phrasebook app (e.g. Google Translate offline)
- Start convos with **"Συγνώμη, μιλάτε Αγγλικά"** (Excuse me, do you speak English?)
- Greeks appreciate effort – even broken Greek gets smiles
