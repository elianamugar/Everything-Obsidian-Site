the first worldwide intergovernmental organization established on January 10, 1920, following the [Paris Peace Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_Nations) to maintain world peace through diplomacy, disarmament, and collective security. Headquartered in Geneva, it acted as a precursor to the United Nations but ultimately failed to prevent World War II, officially dissolving on April 18, 1946.
