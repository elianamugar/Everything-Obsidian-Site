Working title: *She's Got A Way*.
- I like this because it has 2 meanings:
	1) It implies the subject has a way out of a situation.
	2) If said out loud, it also sound's like *She's Got Away*. This implies that some woman has gotten away from the subject, or the subject has gotten away.
- Inspired by the unreleased Chappell Roan *The Subway* song, where the lyrics towards the end echo and repeats itself:
	- *She's got, she's got a way
	  She's got a way, she's got a way
	  She got, she got away*
