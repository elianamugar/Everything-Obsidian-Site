**This is the Last Will and Testament of me, Alexander Wentworth, Earl of Marchford.**

I commend my soul into the hands of Almighty God, trusting in His mercy; and as to the worldly estate wherewith I am entrusted, I dispose thereof as follows.

I declare that I have but one child, born of my lawful marriage, being my only issue and true heir in blood; and it is my earnest desire that such child shall succeed to all the rights, dignities, interests and possessions which by law may descend or accrue.

And as to all such manors, lordships, lands, tenements, hereditaments and real estates whatsoever, whether in possession, reversion, remainder or expectancy, and over which I possess disposing power, together with all monies, securities, goods, chattels and personal estate not herein otherwise bequeathed,

**I do hereby give, devise, and bequeath the same unto my said only child, to have and to hold for his natural life without impeachment of waste; and after such decease, to such child or children of my said child, and in such shares and manner, as my said child shall by deed or last will appoint.**

And in default of such appointment, to the lawful issue of my said child for ever.

And I further declare it to be my settled will that no person whatsoever shall disturb or call into question the right of my said child to enter into full possession of the estates and interests herein devised immediately upon my decease.

In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal this seventh day of June in the year of Our Lord one thousand eight hundred and fifteen.
