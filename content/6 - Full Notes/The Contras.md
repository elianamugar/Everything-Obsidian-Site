# The Contras

1980 was an election year with Reagan as the opposition to the Carter presidency. During Reagan’s campaign, his team reached out to Iran, telling Iran to release the 52 hostages from the Iran Hostage Crisis under the Carter presidency once Reagan wins. Iran agrees to this, even though Reagan’s condemnation of Iran was one of the focal points of his campaign trail. After Reagan’s win, they fulfilled the promise by selling weapons to Israel (which was a public affair due to the Camp David Accords, everyone knew about it) and then Israel would secretly sell the weapons to Iran. Then, another hostage crisis occurs in Lebanon, and Reagan offers the same deal: if you release the hostages, we’ll pay you with weapons. The US gained illegal profits from these weapons deals with Iran and had to store it in a secret Swiss bank account.

In Nicaragua, the family of dictators were overthrown by the Sandinistas, a communist-backed group. During the Carter presidency, Carter rejected the family but then didn’t get involved with the Sandinistas' overthrow because it wasn’t a worthwhile relationship. During his campaign, Reagan condemned Carter for his weakness, saying he wasn’t strong against communism. The Contras were a rebel group in opposition of the Sandinistas. Reagan pressured Carter during the campaign trail to support the Contras, but Carter refused because Carter said the Contras were brutal and unpopular, which went against Carter’s morals. After Carter’s presidential loss to Reagan, Reagan begins to fund the Contras but Congress cuts it off because they were actually brutal drug runners. Despite Congress, Reagan tried every way to keep funding the Contras (Reagan’s strong on rollback of communism). In 1985, the money from the secret Swiss bank account was what Reagan used to fund the Contras illegally. The Sandinista government shot down a CIA plane carrying this illegal money and the affair blew up.
