# "I Love You" by Fontaines DC sounds like The Smiths

Listening to ["I Love You" by Fontaines DC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ty9Pcg3qrmU), I'm reminded of The Smiths. I had a brief period last year where I delved into the short discography of The Smiths and this song in particular mixes Johnny Marr's jangle guitar and an opening bass line that seems reminiscent of Andy Rourke with Morrissey's lyrical angst.

I looked up to see if anyone had a similar thought on this song, and I found [this Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/NameThatSong/comments/1rbfjfz/help_me_find_this_song_that_sounds_like_the/):

>I can't remember if it goes "i hate you i love you i love you" But it's very either Tears for fears, The smiths, or The Cure. I think i heard it on a edit somewhere and it sounds very 80's british what ever style tears for fears and the smiths are?

And I couldn't sum it up any more than that.
