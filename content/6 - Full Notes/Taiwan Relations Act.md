# Taiwan Relations Act

After Triangulation, Kissinger recognized “one China” as the People’s Republic of China, instead of Taiwan. The US still had relations with Taiwan, even though the US recognized communist China. In 1979, when Deng Xiaoping took over after Mao Zedong, Xiaoping believed that communism was a failed economic system. During the same year, Carter invited Xiaoping to finalize a treaty for opening up China and US relations, setting up exchange programs, exchanging ambassadors etc. Taiwan was worried about the opening of China-US relations because there can’t be 2 ambassadors for one China. In April 1979, the Taiwan Relations Act established a relationship between US and Taiwan through trade, cultural ties, scientific, and education ties. The most important aspect of the Act was that the US could still sell weapons to Taiwan and that the reunification of China must be peaceful or else the US will step in. This demonstrated “strategic ambiguity” which was the US’s policy of intentional uncertainty of obligation towards Taiwan.
