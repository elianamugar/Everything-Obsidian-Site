# The devil must exist to keep balance (yin-yang)

Yin and Yang are a fundamental concept in Chinese philosophy, representing a duality of opposite yet complementary forces that are interconnected and interdependent. They are not simply opposites, but rather they exist within each other, and their interaction is essential for balance and harmony.

In The Brothers Karamazov by Fyodor Dostoevsky, the devil meets with Ivan, explaining his role in humanity's eyes. In summary, the devil says he is not evil but maintains the role of *seeming* to be an evil force to keep the balance and harmony in humanity (in fact, he doesn't even want to do this "job" but he does it out of his sense of duty and morality) ([[The Brothers Karamazov (1880) by Fyodor Dostoevsky#587-8]]). For, if there does not exist evil, then humanity would not know what goodness is. 

So to Ivan's question of "Does God exist?" Well, it's not so simple. If the devil exists, then it does not necessarily mean God exists. The devil says that he doesn't know if God exists, but the devil works to represent the evil force, to be disliked, so that man has some idea of the opposite force (which may come in the shape of "God").
