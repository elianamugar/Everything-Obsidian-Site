# Marx's concepts in Wall Street (1987)

Marx’s idea of class conflict (ex: Carl Fox - proletariat working class vs. Wall Street traders - bourgeoise) meant that there were always social classes against each other. One class always physically owned the means of production, while the other ruled through the _idea_ of owning the means of production. Marx saw surplus value as theft from the working class, but it was profit for capitalists. For example, in _Wall Street_, the stock traders sell their shares for profit more than the workers at Blue Star and Anacott Steel earn for wages. Economic determination of culture simply means all of culture is determined by economic relations. In _Wall Street_, when Bud Fox gets thrown into the world of high end stock trading, Gekko first introduces him to Darien Taylor. Besides a charming girl, the culture of highly fashionable suits, drugs, and expensive apartments for rent on 5th Ave. became Bud Fox’s culture. Bud Fox became immersed in the culture of high end stock traders.
