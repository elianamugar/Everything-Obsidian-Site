# Religion in war

The use of religion in war has been around for a long time, since the times of the Greeks and the Romans, and perhaps even earlier. In Herodotus's *The Histories*, he quotes Xerxes' conference following the conquest of Egypt. 

Xerxes states the following:
>We Persians have a way of living, which I have inherited from my predecessors and propose to follow. I have learned from my elders that ever since Cyrus deposed Astyages and we took over from the Medes the sovereign power we now possess, we have never yet remained inactive. **This is God's guidance, and it is by following it that we have gained our great prosperity.**

Here, Xerxes lays out that God leads them into battle and that it is only right they follow along his plans. As a result, they have won the battle, which boosts soldiers' morale. 

Of course, this is not the only instance that religion has been used as a justification for war, but these are the early seeds into the [[Divine Right of Kings]].
