# Camp David Accords

After the [[Six Day War]] and [[Yom Kippur War]]s, in 1978, Carter invited el-Sadat and Begin to Camp David to broker a peace treaty. At Camp David, both sides couldn’t get along, so Carter had to deal with each side individually. El-Sadat voiced his concern that he was risking Arab betrayal by signing a peace treaty--el-Sadat was betraying the ultimate message of pan-Arabism. In March 1979, the Camp David Accords were officially signed, which stated that Israel would give back the Sinai Peninsula (gained from the Egypt and Israel wars) and el-Sadat would recognize Israel (a betrayal to the Arab world, against Pan-Arabism). On top of that, Carter added another deal: Israel and Egypt would become the top 2 receivers of US assistance, which implied a US-Egyptian alliance. After the Camp David Accords, there was an issue with the Gaza Strip--both sides didn’t want it. The Palestinian Arabs hate Israel due to history, and they also hated el-Sadat due to the betrayal to the Arab world of recognizing Israel. El-Sadat says Israel has to negotiate with the Palestinians to create an independent state, which Begin agrees with, but doesn’t set a date for doing so. Because of the Arab world’s hatred towards el-Sadat after the signing of the Camp David Accords, el-Sadat was assassinated.
