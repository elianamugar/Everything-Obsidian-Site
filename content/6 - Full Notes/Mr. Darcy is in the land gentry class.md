# Mr. Darcy is in the land gentry class

In *Pride and Prejudice* (1813) by Jane Austen, Mr. Darcy earns £10,000 per year (or per annum, to use the words from the book). 

In 2023, that amount is roughly £824,200 which is equal to $1,089,520! This was just based off the 4% interest from his investments (passive income), not the rents from his estates ([source](https://blog.gale.com/deconstructing-mr-darcy-just-how-rich-was-he/)). His inheritance/net worth would've been so much more, so it's understandable why Mrs. Bennet was really egging Elizabeth on to marry him!

So, what is landed gentry? It's a social class, specifically in Britain, made up of wealthy landowners who often resided on estates and lived off rental income ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landed_gentry)). They weren't nobility, so the landed gentry were still ranked below nobility, but were still considered upper class. Hereditary gentry still exists today, though the decline of the gentry largely began with the 1870s agricultural depression.

Although it was never explicit, it was heavily implied through the subtext and context of Austen's novel that Mr. Darcy is a part of the land gentry class.
