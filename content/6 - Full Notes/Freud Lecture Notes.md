# Notes
Freud’s background
- Died before WWII, Austrian
- Examines impulses of human species toward life and death
- Wrote book between world wars
Freud’s Book, _Civilization and Its Discontents_ - Background
- About life and death
- Philosophical ruminations of Freud’s interest in non-scientific questions
- Strict Freudians argue that he should’ve confined his clinical techniques to neurotic patients, not whole human species
- Sociological issues were always implicit in his work
- 1970s: Argentina run by military junta - anti-Semitic bc of who they hated
	- Buenos Aires journalist converses with them, military junta hated 3 intellectuals: Einstein, Marx, Freud (physical, society, ourselves)
	- Nazis hated all 3 men as well
- 1930s: Nazis rise to power, burn Freud’s books (still living in Vienna, Austria)
- 1938: Freud flees Vienna
Freud’s offensive ideas
- Infant sexuality
	- Freud’s belief goes against traditional belief in childhood innocence
	- Freud: all human beings go through sexual development, where they look for their pleasure
		- First stage: oral sexuality, sucking on things, teething, etc.
		- Anal stage: pooping, potty training, etc.
		- Latency period: no interest in sexuality at all (boys/girls gross)
		- Puberty
- Oedipus/Electra complexes
	- Freud: outlines stage of mother-daughter, father-son for love of parent of opposite sex
- Emphasis on sexual motivation in general
	- Traditional moralists don’t really go for this, sexuality hand-in-hand with sin
- Subtle principle: abnormal reveals the normal
	- Every psychological disorder is an extreme form of something quite common
- Freud’s work resisted by conservative Viennese society + medical establishment, Nazis
	- Freud looked into the individual mind, but Nazis didn’t want individuals (collective to contribute to society)
	- Simone de Beauvoir’s _Second Sex_ attacks Freud’s ideas - very feminist
		- 1920s: ironically saw Freud as a “liberator” of women
	- American psychologists, neuroscientists, etc.
Freud’s Version of Substructure and Superstructure
- _The Conscious Mind_ - above the water line
	- aware
- _The Unconscious Mind_ - below the water line
	- Unaware, Freud focuses on this
	- Our species’ self-love
	- Believed his idea of subconscious robs us of the idea of self-control
		- Most of Western ethical thinking based on assumption of knowing our motives and principles
	- Psychoanalysis method to know ourselves → delve into the unconscious
		- How to access below the water line?
		- Attempts: hypnosis → dream analysis → couch talk therapy
- Freud: retain how to do a psychoanalysis of life
- A great culture hero - examines inward life in search of truth
	- Freud aimed at an objective account of human nature (scientifically)
	- How to get to objective truth of subconscious?
		- Examines subjectivity, what we feel deeply inside ourselves as individuals
		- Examines own psyche, childhood, dreams, subconscious
			- Shatters his own defenses before dealing with his patients
Contents of _Civilization and Its Discontents_
- Unconscious drives
	- Aggression and destruction
	- How conscience gets created
	- Where guilt comes from; civilization rests on guilt
- Freud wants to make a science of subjectivity
- Oedipus/Electra complex derive from Greek tragedy
	- Freud’s work can be regarded as a vast analytic poem aimed at telling the story of our species’ inner life (dialectical lyric)
	- Freud as a poet: Steam Power
Steam Engine as Model of Freud’s Concept of Personality
![[SteamEngineAsModelOfFreudsConceptOfPersonality.png]]
- **Id:** underlying our personality, sexual energy driving us all the time, undisciplined, hedonistic
- **Ego:** conscious self, pressure builds up, but repressed by demands of society and our own conscience
- **Superego:** must release pressure, sublimation (take energy and release to do work), demands of society want us to be useful members of society
- **Defense mechanisms:** things we do to prevent from blowing up, let off steam
	- Displacement, projection, substitution, denial
- Freud considers if the pressures of life will result in the boiler blowing up (individual → civilization)
	- Don’t take Freud’s ideas too seriously
Freudian Slip
- When you use the wrong word, but reveals what’s really on your mind
- Inward life of human kind
- Disagrees with Marx (believes insides determined by outside)
	- Freud believes outer world reflects our inner life
