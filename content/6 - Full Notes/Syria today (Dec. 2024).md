# Syria today (Dec. 2024)
- Hayat Tahrir al-Sham ([[HTS]], or the "Organization for the Liberation of the Levant") is the militant group that led the offensive against Assad, led by Abu Mohammed al-Golani/Ahmad al-Sharaa (former al-Nusra commander)
	- Original Jabhat al-Nusra (or in some articles, they're seen as al-Nusra Front), set up an offensive against Assad in 2012
	- 2013 - Jabhat al-Nusra pledges allegiance to al-Qaeda
	- Free Syrian Army (funded by US + Europeans + Turks + Saudis) were against Jabhat al-Nusra, fearing jihadist ideology
	- 2016 - al-Nusra breaks ties with [[al-Qaeda]], taking name of HTS, merging with other factions later
		- UN, US, UK, etc. consider HTS as an al-Qaeda affiliat, frequently referring it to al-Nusra Front and also consider it as a terrorist group
		- But HTS defeated al-Qaeda (most likely extremists?) and [[ISIL]]
- 2020 - Turkey + Russia broker a ceasefire
- Turkey-backed opposition fighters are battling U.S.-allied Kurdish forces in the north
- ISIL is still active in remote areas
- It's claimed that al-Golani/al-Sharaa "embraces pluralism and religious tolerance" ([PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/syrian-government-falls-to-rebel-offensive-in-stunning-end-to-assad-familys-50-year-rule#:~:text=embraces%20pluralism%20and%20religious%20tolerance))
